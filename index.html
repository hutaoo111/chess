How the Game Works

The six pieces

♚King

Moves one square in any direction. Protect it at all costs.

♛Queen

Moves in any direction, any distance. The most powerful piece.

♜Rook

Moves in straight lines: forwards, backwards and sideways.

♝Bishop

Slides diagonally and always stays on its own colour.

♞Knight

Jumps in an “L” shape, even over other pieces.

♟Pawn

Walks forward, captures diagonally, and can become a queen.

The board

Two players take turns. The board has 64 squares, and each player starts with 16 pieces.

The goal

Trap the opponent’s king so it has no escape. This final move is called checkmate.

What Chess Teaches Us About Life

Sixty-four squares, and lessons that go far beyond the board.

♞Think ahead

Every move has a consequence. Chess trains strategic thinking, like preparing for an exam instead of studying the night before.

♜Stay calm under pressure

When the clock is ticking, panic loses games. The same is true in tests and job interviews.

♚Own your decisions

There is no luck and no one to blame. Every win and every loss comes from my own choices.

♟Grow from setbacks

A small pawn can become a queen. Failure is not the end, because patience turns mistakes into progress.

My Advice for Beginners

♙Master the basics

You should learn how every piece moves before you play seriously.

♗Make it a habit

You should practice regularly. Twenty minutes a day beats three hours once a week.

♖Study your defeats

You should review every game you lose, because each mistake is a free lesson.

♚Keep going

You should not give up after losing. Every great player has lost many games.
